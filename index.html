Questions

Is it free?

Yes. CommitCheck is free to use.

Do I need to log in or connect my bank?

No. There is no login and no bank connection. You type in your own salary dates, cash in hand and move costs, and nothing you type is saved or sent anywhere.

Is this financial advice?

No, it only checks the timing of your own payments; it does not recommend loans, investments or tax decisions.

Who is it for?

Salaried people planning to move flats in the next two months, especially if the deposit, advance rent, brokerage and packers are due before your next salary or before your old deposit comes back.

Why I built this

I built CommitCheck after a real move went wrong: the payments for the new flat fell due before the money to cover them arrived. This is the check I wish I had run before signing.
